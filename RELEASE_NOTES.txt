--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INCLUDED IN THIS VERSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Dual Character Selection: Choose between Darrel and Darla with custom 4-directional locomotion animations and tailored dialogue portraits.
* Museum Hub & Room Navigation: Exploration across the Main Lobby, Left and Right museum wings, and 12 historical artifact exhibition rooms.
* Alterworld Portal & Corrupted Realm: Unlocking the lobby portal runes to traverse into the gloomy Alterworld (AltLobby, AltLeftHallway, AltRightHallway).
* Turn-Based Educational Quiz Combat: Full combat arenas across all 12 rooms featuring historical trivia to weaken corrupted monsters and recover stolen national relics.
* Artifact Bag & Restoration System: Dedicated bag inventory modal and pedestal mounting interface allowing players to return recovered relics to their pedestals.
* Plaque Inspection & Clue Tracking: Complete historical plaque reading system with real-time HUD progression counter (e.g. 0/5 to 5/5 studied).
* Hanging Rustic Notification Banner: Animated wooden sign with suspension chains displaying room events, combat alerts, and milestone announcements.
* Cloud Authentication & Persistence: Firebase authentication with real-time cloud data storage alongside clean local PlayerPrefs fallback.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIXED / REFINED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Prevented character from clipping into locked portal area in the Lobby before Room 1 plaque inspection is complete.
* Corrected battle sequence so enemy portrait and health bar only appear after the player actively selects an artifact to challenge.
* Resolved battle notification issues in Battle 2, ensuring that every correct answer and artifact recovery properly displays an on-screen alert.
* Refined bag inventory and restore picker modal layout, removing redundant descriptions and adding high-contrast pixel-art "Place" buttons.
* Standardized button labels and capitalizations ("Place from bag").

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWN ISSUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Audio assets (BGM tracks and sound effects) are temporarily omitted in v0.1.0 to minimize initial WebGL package download size.
* Museum visual restoration stage (lighting and mural decay recovery) is still using static base textures.
* Battle retry flow requires re-entering through the lobby portal rather than an instant rematch prompt.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALLATION / RUNNING INSTRUCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. To play via browser: Visit the published WebGL page at https://nouzen214.github.io/PAMANA/
2. To run locally: Extract "Pamana-v0.1.0.zip", execute "run_webgl.bat", and open "http://localhost:8000/index.html" in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox.
3. Minimum Requirements: Modern desktop web browser supporting WebGL 2.0 and WebAssembly, 4GB RAM, and a stable internet connection for cloud authentication.
